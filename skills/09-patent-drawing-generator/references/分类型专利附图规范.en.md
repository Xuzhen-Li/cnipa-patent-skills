# Drawing rules by invention type

## General rules

- Follow the Chinese patent application documents and electronic-filing requirements in force on the filing date. When a rule is uncertain, record it for review by a patent attorney (专利代理师). Do not hard-code a dimension from memory.
- Use clear black lines on a white background. Avoid color, grayscale rendering, a photographic look, shadows, gradients, and decoration.
- The same object keeps the same mark in every figure. The same mark must not point to different objects. Leader lines are clear and do not cross key structure.
- Figure numbers, view names, section symbols, detail regions, and states must be defined in the view plan.
- Do not add dimensions, scales, material textures, or long explanatory sentences unless they are actually needed to express the technical solution.
- After scaling to the application page, outlines, connections, arrows, symbols, and marks must still be readable.

## Machines and physical structures

- Prefer an overall view, orthographic views, sections, an exploded view, key detail enlargements, and necessary working-state views.
- Hatch direction and spacing should distinguish neighboring parts. A section must not change the shape or connections of the parts that are not cut.
- An exploded view changes only the separation distance along the assembly axis. It does not change part orientation, assembly order, or interface shape.
- A motion-state view must state the source of the state, the fixed parts, the moving parts, the direction of motion, and the limit positions.
- Formal figures are exported from CAD or a vector engineering source. A generative bitmap may be used only as a viewpoint prototype.

## Circuits and control systems

- Use consistent, interpretable electrical symbols and port names. Keep the direction of power, signals, ground, and control connections consistent.
- Module names, interfaces, and signal names must correspond across the overall block diagram, the schematic or connection diagram, and the control flow.
- Do not change a node connection, port ownership, or signal direction in order to adjust the layout.

## Software and computing systems

- Keep system architecture, functional modules, data flow, flowcharts, and sequence diagrams distinct. Avoid mixing several semantic levels in one figure.
- Modules, data objects, inputs and outputs, processing steps, and the actor that performs them must match the claims (权利要求书) and the description (说明书).
- Every arrow must have a clear meaning. Do not mix control flow, data flow, and call relationships without a label.

## Methods and process flows

- Step numbers, order, parallel relationships, conditional branches, loops, inputs and outputs, and exception paths must be readable.
- Do not add a mandatory step that is not in the claims or the fact source.
- When an equipment drawing and a process flow both exist, equipment marks, material direction, and step names must correspond.

## Chemical and biological technology

- Chemical structures, reaction schemes, and biological-sequence diagrams should be drawn with a professional chemistry or bioinformatics tool. Do not replace an exact structure with a generative image.
- Check atoms, bond types, substitution positions, stereochemistry, reactants and products, conditions, sample groups, and flow direction.
- Use a curve, a spectrum, or an experimental apparatus only when expressing the technical solution truly requires it and the original data can be traced. Do not generate a fictional experimental figure.

## Mixed types

- Build a professional master for each type, then use an interface table to align parts and modules, signals and materials, steps, and states.
- The same fact in a cross-type view has only one master-data definition. Do not let the mechanical figure, the circuit figure, and the flow figure each invent a name.
