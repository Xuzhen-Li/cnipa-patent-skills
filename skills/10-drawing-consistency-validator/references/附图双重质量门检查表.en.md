# Dual quality-gate checklist for drawings

## How to use it

Finish the substantive consistency gate first, then the formal compliance gate. For each item record `pass`, `warning`, or `fail`, the evidence, the confidence, the reviewer, and the review time. If a key item cannot be observed, do not pass it by default.

## Substantive consistency gate

- Every figure was actually opened, and the files match the hash list.
- Every part, module, step, or chemical structure can be traced to the fact source and to the claims (权利要求书).
- Names, marks, counts, shapes or symbols, connections, positions or levels, directions, inside/outside relationships, and states agree across figures.
- The Figure 1 (图1) overall master matches the deterministic engineering source, and the other views are declared derivatives of it.
- Differences for sections, exploded views, detail enlargements, states, and flow branches were defined in advance and have a factual basis.
- Technical relationships that the claims need to show are expressed. The images do not add necessary structure that is absent from the claims and the fact source.
- OCR output, marks seen by eye, and the master data agree.
- Outline, interface, or position changes found by the difference overlay have been explained.

## Formal compliance gate

- Figure numbers are continuous and match the citation plan in the description (说明书). The abstract figure (摘要附图) comes from the same passed set.
- Black lines and a white background. Outlines, hatching, arrows, symbols, and marks are clear.
- No color, grayscale rendering, shadows, gradients, photographic background, decoration, or watermark.
- One mark names one object, and the same object keeps the same mark. Leader-line ends are clear and do not cover key structure.
- Text is used only when a flow, a block diagram, or the technical expression truly needs it, and the terms match the body text.
- No unnecessary dimensions, scales, material textures, slogans, or long explanatory sentences.
- Resolution, margins, page orientation, and readability after scaling meet the electronic-filing requirements that currently apply.
- Every formal figure has a reproducible engineering source, an export record, and a hash that has not been altered.

## Must be reviewed by a person

- A key structure is occluded, the image is blurred, or different viewpoints make a reliable judgment impossible.
- OCR and the marks seen by eye disagree.
- The difference overlay suggests an interface, outline, or position change, but the reason is unclear.
- A chemical structure, stereochemistry, a circuit symbol, or another specialist graphic is outside reliable automatic recognition.
- The applicable form rule has a version difference or an uncertain interpretation.
